# Contract: `render.material.v2` and `render.shader-artifacts.v1` (first slice)

Modules: `render.material` (layer 3), `render.shader-library` and
`render.resources` (layer 2)
Suites: `unittests/rendertest/core/material/test_material.cpp`,
`unittests/rendertest/core/shaderlib/test_shaderlib.cpp`,
`unittests/rendertest/core/resources/test_resources.cpp`
Rows: R88 (RFC 0016 K4; this is its first slice)

| Clause | Obligation |
| --- | --- |
| M1 | A family's parameters take std140-style offsets; textures take slots |
| M2 | Duplicate families or parameters, and families of more than four bind groups, fail |
| M3 | A family's missing capability is named |
| M4 | Parameter blocks start at the defaults; setters check name and type; the revision rises only on a real change |
| S1 | The artifact store rejects duplicate keys and empty artifacts |
| S2 | Permutation keys are a bijection over the declared axes |
| S3 | A recipe resolves only to the requested artifact format, failing by key otherwise, and survives a move |
| R1–R5 | Resource caches upload staged bytes, end in their use usage, keep a replaced resource live until the replacing submission completes, and release everything at teardown |
| R6 | `TextureCache::StageMips` (2026-09-28, for the Hammer viewports' mipmaps): a mip chain of 1 to `mipLevels` levels, each `max(1, size >> m)` texels, stages as one upload with one buffer-to-texture copy per level from a single staging buffer (16-byte aligned offsets), and every level lands in its mip. A level of the wrong size, no level, or more levels than the texture has fails and stages nothing. `Stage` is `StageMips` with mip 0 alone |

## K4 clauses (2026-09-28)

Suites: `test_material.cpp` (`render.material.v2`), `test_material_sensitivity.cpp`
(`render.material.v2.sensitivity`), the corpus (`render.material.vmt-corpus`,
`.sensitivity`, `.asan`) and the artifacts (`render.shader-artifacts`,
`.sensitivity`). The shared clauses are `material_conformance.h`.

| Clause | Obligation |
| --- | --- |
| F1–F4 | The VMT mapping defines the core families `lightmapped`, `vertexlit`, `unlit` and `pbr` (in that order); each registers with the four groups; the schema is exactly the GPU key rows, PBR's is RFC 0007's; defaults come from the rows; the legacy shader table is populated and its aliases resolve |
| K1 | Every GPU key row's value lands in the parameter its key names (the key without its `$`) in the built-in schema. A row mapped to another parameter, or read with another kind, is caught (sensitivity S1, S2) |
| R1 | A derived copy (`ParameterBlockCopy`) is not current after any change to its block and equals it (bytes, textures, revision) after a refresh; refreshing a current copy copies nothing. A copy never refreshed after a change, and one whose revision moves without its bytes, are caught (S3, S4) |
| V1–V10 | VMT reading follows the material system: `[$SYMBOL]` tags, `cond?` variables, the profile's fallback block, first definitions (a conditional one replaces), aliases, the legacy family keeping every variable, `subrect`, unknown shaders by name, texture prefixes, enumerations, braced colors, scalars filling vectors, unreadable values reported, proxies, editor and metadata keys, unmapped keys, malformed text, the end of input closing blocks |
| P1–P3 | Patches: includes resolve in order; insert adds and overwrites; replace overwrites only existing keys; missing, absent and more than ten nested includes fail by status |
| B1–B3 | PBRMetalRough: required parameters are named when absent; the fallback reference must be well formed, exist and not name the material itself |
| A1–A2 | `ApplyValues` writes a material's values at their offsets and raises the revision; a block of another family refuses them |
| C1 | Every VMT of the Portal and Portal 2 corpus imports or is reported with its status; the family counts, unsupported counts, unsupported materials and the family digest equal `quality/fixtures/render-material/vmt-corpus-v1.json`; a seeded wrong shader mapping and a missing VMT are caught |
| A-ART | Every shader the backend ships, and every family program, builds a SPIR-V and a GLSL 4.50 artifact (or a declared exclusion); reflection matches `render/shaders/layouts.json`; a family declares at most four groups; the generated headers agree with the regenerators and the compiler (none is committed) |
| X1–X4 | Texture transforms: `$basetexturetransform`, `$detailtexturetransform` and `$envmapmasktransform` in every legacy-derived family, `$bumptransform` in `lightmapped` and `vertexlit`, `$basetexturetransform2` and `$blendmasktransform` in `lightmapped` are `kTransform` parameters: rows 0 and 1 of the 4x4 (8 floats, 16-byte aligned, the identity by default), as shaders read them. Both of the material system's forms import (16 row-major numbers, or `center u v scale u v rotate degrees translate u v` composed as T(c + t) Rz S T(−c)); a malformed one keeps the identity with a diagnostic. `MapVariables` maps a shader's variables exactly as `ImportVmt` maps a VMT's (the legacy frontend's entry for bound materials). A family refuses a transform it does not claim, by name |
| PC | Proxy corpus, the frontend side (`render.material.proxies.core-portal`, `.core-portal2`): with the render core composed, `RenderMaterialBlocks001` (`public/render/legacy/material_blocks.h`, `render/legacy/material_blocks.cpp`) turns a bound material's variables, as the material system prints them, into its family's parameter block through `MapVariables` and `ApplyValues`, and reports the block read back. For every proxy fixture material and pass, after its proxies bind, each block parameter equals the legacy variable its key names (textures by name, flags and integers by the importer's rules, floats, vectors and texture transforms within 1e-5); legacy-family materials have no block. Composition clause P5 covers the interface; the comparator's seeded block faults are in `render.material.proxies.comparator` |
| F-UNLIT | `render.family.unlit`: the `unlit` family (`unlit_family.h`) claims UnlitGeneric's base texture, `$color`/`$alpha`, vertex color and alpha, alpha test, and translucent or additive blending; it refuses any other parameter set away from its default, by name. Each case of `quality/fixtures/legacy-shaders/families/unlit.vdf`, imported and claimed, drawn with the family into an sRGB target with the D3D9 half-pixel shift a legacy draw carries, matches the legacy port's pixels within 2 levels (`quality/fixtures/render-families/unlit-port-v1.vdf`, recorded from a port run judged against the retail D3D9 bytecode). Source's gamma rules apply: `$color` through the 2.2 table (1 from 0.95), vertex color through pow 2.2; destination alpha is kept for translucent and alpha-tested draws (D17); the alpha-test reference is the legacy one (`detail::AlphaTestReference`: `$alphatestreference` above zero, else the default state's 0.7, held as a byte). Seeded: `$vertexcolor` packed off is caught |
| F-LIGHTMAPPED | `render.family.lightmapped`: the `lightmapped` family (`lightmapped_family.h`) claims LightmappedGeneric's and WorldVertexTransition's base texture, `$color`/`$alpha`, vertex color, vertex alpha blending, alpha test and translucency; it refuses any other parameter set away from its default, by name (bump maps, `$basetexture2`, env maps, `$additive`). The family's draws are points of the one surface program (`surface_program.h`, RFC 0016 K11): the lightmap page and its sampler are the draw's bind group (role `kDraw`, with neutral model lighting); the material group holds the program's constants and textures (an unread slot takes the neutral texture); the frame group holds the frame's terms and an unread split-sum slot. Each case of `quality/fixtures/legacy-shaders/families/lightmapped.vdf`, drawn as `F-UNLIT` draws its cases, matches the legacy port's pixels within 2 levels (`quality/fixtures/render-families/lightmapped-port-v1.vdf`). The port's rules apply: the tint is `$color` times the lightmap scale 2^2.2 with no gamma conversion; vertex color is used unconverted; on the vertex fast path the vertex alpha replaces the modulation alpha, and without `$vertexcolor` `$alpha` applies twice; the alpha-test reference is `F-UNLIT`'s. Seeded: `$color` gamma-converted is caught |
| F-VERTEXLIT | `render.family.vertexlit`: the `vertexlit` family (`vertexlit_family.h`) claims VertexLitGeneric's base texture, `$color`/`$alpha`, alpha test, translucency and `$halflambert`; `$vertexcolor` and `$vertexalpha` are accepted and their vertex data ignored, as the port ignores it for VertexLitGeneric. It refuses any other parameter set away from its default, by name (bump maps, env maps, detail, self-illumination, `$phong`, rim lights, light warps, `$additive`). The draw's lighting is its bind group (role `kDraw`, `LightingRequest`): Source's model lighting as `PackSourceModelLighting` packs it; the material group holds the constants, the base texture and its sampler; the draw constants are world-to-clip and object-to-world (the `FamilyDrawConstants` prefix). Each case of `quality/fixtures/legacy-shaders/families/vertexlit.vdf`, drawn as `F-UNLIT` draws its cases with its ambient cube and lights, matches the legacy port's pixels within 2 levels (`quality/fixtures/render-families/vertexlit-port-v1.vdf`, a `-vklegacyvertexlit` run). The port's rules apply: per-vertex `DoLighting` with static control flow (the lights in order, then the ambient cube), `$color` through the 2.2 table, `$alpha` times the base alpha; the draw blends for `$translucent`, `$vertexalpha` or `$alpha` below one; the alpha-test reference is quantized to eight bits; destination alpha is kept for blended and alpha-tested draws (D17). Seeded: `$halflambert` dropped is caught |
| F-FRAME-FOG | `render.family.lightmapped` fog case: the view's fog is a frame term of the `lightmapped` frame group (`SurfaceFrame::fogColor/fogParams/fogMisc`, from `legacy::CorePassFog` captured at the slot). Range fog follows common_ps_fxc.h: `clamp(min(maxDensity, projZ / range - start / range))`, squared, mixed toward the fog color in linear light after the tone-mapping scale and before the output encoding. Height fog uses the water and eye z, and a fully opaque material (`SurfaceConstants::state.x`) writes its factor to alpha, as WRITEWATERFOGTODESTALPHA does. The case (factor 0.25) is within 2 levels of the formula; with the factor left unsquared it fails. `state.y` decodes the vertex color from gamma per vertex (pow 2.2) for UnlitGeneric's degenerate case and the editor preview; LightmappedGeneric's vertex color stays unconverted |
| F-PREVIEW | `render.world.null` P1: `ProgramResolver::ResolvePreview` is the editor's preview of any material. By contract it approximates and never claims: the base texture times `$color` and `$alpha`, with the material's blend (`$translucent` or `$vertexalpha` alpha, `$additive`), alpha test and `detail::AlphaTestReference`, the vertex color on and the lighting fixed at one. Every variable it doesn't read (metadata aside) is listed in `Preview::ignored`. Game passes never use it (RFC 0016 "No escape hatches"); strict `Resolve` of the same material still refuses |
| G1–G11 | `render.material.programs`: `GroupResidency` (the one mechanism under `MaterialPrograms` and `DrawGroups`, `material_programs.h`) makes a group only after `RecordUploads` and only while every texture it names is in the `TextureCache`; a replaced texture (its revision rose) gives a new group naming the new texture and the old group is released behind the token; eviction and `Remove` take the group away; a replaced request uploads its constants again; requests without a layout, programs without a pipeline, with stride 0, or with draw constants over 128 bytes or not a multiple of four are refused and change nothing; samplers are shared by description; destruction releases every object. A program carries its pipeline, vertex stride, draw-constant size, the draw layout it reads and its material group; a draw group its layout and group. A family's `Request` (unlit's first) turns a claim and a `TextureCache` name into a `ProgramRequest` |
| F-PBR | `render.family.pbr`: the `pbr` family (`pbr_family.h`) claims RFC 0007 PBRMetalRough's `$basetexture`, `$mraotexture`, `$bumpmap`, `$emissiontexture` and `$emissionscale` (`$fallbackmaterial` is accepted); it refuses environment maps, alpha test, translucency, clear coat and glass by name, and a block without both required textures bound. The family's draws are the pbr point of the one surface program (`surface_program.h`, RFC 0016 K11): the frame group holds the frame's terms (the eye is the view's) and the split-sum table (`SplitSumTable`), the draw group Source's model lighting (`PackSourceModelLighting` in `model_lighting.h`: ambient cube, up to four lights sorted spot, point, directional, with `SetLight`'s cone), the material group the program's constants and seven texture/sampler pairs (the slots the point does not read take the neutral texture); the draw constants are world-to-clip and object-to-world (the `FamilyDrawConstants` prefix). The BRDF is `render/shaders/common/pbr_brdf.glsl`, the one GLSL copy. Each case of the recorded native `pbr-model` run (`quality/fixtures/render-families/pbr-port-v1.vdf`, `family_port_pixels.py record-model`: the harness's own inputs and the port's pixels where its BRDF oracle judges) matches the port within 2 levels. A directional light shines along its direction. Seeded: the normal map ignored is caught |
